Underfitting: When a model is too simple to capture the underlying structure of the data.

Accuracy: The ratio of correct predictions made by the model to the total number of predictions.

Chatbot: AI software capable of conversing with users in natural language.

Hallucination: When AI models confidently produce false, illogical, or non-existent information.

Token: The smallest meaningful units into which text is divided for AI processing.

Prompt: The input or instruction written by a user to get a response from an AI model.

GPU (Graphics Processing Unit): A hardware unit that accelerates the parallel computations of AI models.
