
Algorithm: A sequence of logical steps followed to solve a specific problem.

Parameter: Variables learned by the model during training that determine its predictive power.

Supervised Learning: A method where the model is guided to the correct result using labeled data.

Unsupervised Learning: A method aimed at discovering hidden structures and patterns in unlabeled data.

Reinforcement Learning: The process of an agent learning the best strategy through a reward and punishment mechanism.

Computer Vision (CV): The capability of machines to interpret and understand the visual world (images/video).

Generative AI (GenAI): A type of artificial intelligence capable of creating new content such as text, images, or audio.

Transfer Learning: The transfer of knowledge learned in one task to a different but related task.

Fine-Tuning: The optimization of a pre-trained model for a specific task using a smaller dataset.

Overfitting: A situation where the model memorizes the data and fails to perform on new, unseen data.
